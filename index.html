The House of Heritage

Founded in 2018 in the heart of historical Ashgabat, Eagle & Vine emerged from a profound desire to merge centuries-old Eastern craftsmanship with timeless European old-money silhouettes.

What started as a private archive of bespoke tailored garments quickly became a symbol of quiet status, recognized globally for uncompromising fabrics, rigid attention to detail, and limited-run sovereign aesthetics.
